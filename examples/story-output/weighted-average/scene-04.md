## Scene 04 — Weight means how much a rate represents

### Context (not spoken)

- Overall goal: Understand how group sizes determine a combined percentage by reasoning from total passes and total students.
- Before: scene-03 — The larger class needs more influence because the combined question concerns individual students.
- This scene: Introduce weighted averages as a consequence of recovering and combining counts.
- After: scene-05 — The final scene tests the reasoning with equal class sizes.

### Script

Narration: Giving each rate influence according to its group size is called a weighted average. Here, the weights are student counts. Ten times ninety percent recovers nine passes; thirty times fifty percent recovers fifteen. Add those passes, then divide by all forty students. So weighting follows directly from counting. Whenever you combine percentages, ask what each percentage is a percentage of. Those underlying totals tell you how much each rate should contribute.
