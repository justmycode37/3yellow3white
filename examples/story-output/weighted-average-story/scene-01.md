## Scene 01 — The tempting answer

### Context (not spoken)

- Overall goal: Understand how group sizes determine a combined percentage by reasoning from total passes and total students.
- Before: Opening scene — The video opens with two fictional classes, assuming the listener understands percentages.
- This scene: Introduce a tempting average and establish the question of what the combined pass rate measures.
- After: scene-02 — The next scene supplies class sizes so the listener can reason from student counts.

### Script

Narration: Two classes take the same quiz. In our fictional example, ninety percent of Class A passes, compared with fifty percent of Class B. Average those percentages, and you get seventy percent. So did seventy percent of all the students pass? That sounds reasonable, but something is missing: how many students are in each class? We're asking about the share of students who passed across both classes. Every student needs to count equally.
