## Scene 02 — Count the students

### Context (not spoken)

- Overall goal: Understand how group sizes determine a combined percentage by reasoning from total passes and total students.
- Before: scene-01 — The opening established that the combined rate must count each student equally.
- This scene: Use the actual group sizes to let the listener discover the combined pass rate.
- After: scene-03 — The next scene explains why averaging the two percentages produced a different answer.

### Script

Narration: Class A has ten students, and nine pass. That's its ninety percent. Class B has thirty students, and fifteen pass. That's its fifty percent. To find the combined percentage, we can go back to those counts.

Invitation (spoken): How many students passed altogether, and out of how many students? Take a moment to combine the counts and work out the percentage.

Pause: 7s

Reveal (spoken): Nine plus fifteen gives twenty-four passes. Ten plus thirty gives forty students. Twenty-four out of forty is sixty percent. That's the combined pass rate.
