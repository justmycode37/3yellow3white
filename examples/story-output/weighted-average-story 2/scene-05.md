## Scene 05 — When the usual average works

### Context (not spoken)

- Overall goal: Understand how group sizes determine a combined percentage by reasoning from total passes and total students.
- Before: scene-04 — Weighting has been explained as combining successes and the totals behind each percentage.
- This scene: Test the method with equal class sizes and identify when the ordinary average works.
- After: End of video — The video ends with the principle that combined rates come from combined counts.

### Script

Invitation (spoken): One small change: now each class has ten students. Nine pass in Class A, and five pass in Class B. Does seventy percent now describe the combined result? Take a moment to check using the counts.

Pause: 5s

Reveal (spoken): Yes. Fourteen out of twenty students passed: seventy percent. Equal class sizes justify equal influence for the percentages. With unequal sizes, that shortcut isn't reliable. The dependable method is to combine the passes, combine the students, and then calculate the percentage.
