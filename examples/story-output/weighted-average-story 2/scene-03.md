## Scene 03 — What seventy percent assumes

### Context (not spoken)

- Overall goal: Understand how group sizes determine a combined percentage by reasoning from total passes and total students.
- Before: scene-02 — Adding passes and students established a combined rate of sixty percent.
- This scene: Explain how equal influence for the classes misrepresents their unequal numbers of students.
- After: scene-04 — The next scene names weighting and connects it to the counting method.

### Script

Narration: Why did seventy percent miss? Averaging ninety and fifty gives each class half the influence. But Class B contains three times as many students as Class A. Its lower pass rate therefore needs three times as much influence on the combined result. That's why sixty percent sits closer to fifty than to ninety. The arithmetic that produced seventy wasn't broken; it gave equal importance to classes when our question gave equal importance to students.
