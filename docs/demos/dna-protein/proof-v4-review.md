## Major findings resolved

1. **Active site belongs to solid enzyme volume.** Gold domains occupy the space behind the strands; the original large purple window is gone. The construction no longer reads as a torus.
2. **The scene is a tight asymmetric close-up.** The upper strand is near the top crop on the left and the lower strand rises strongly toward the right. RNA enters from the left and extends toward the selected site. The former centered symmetrical ladder is gone.
3. **Medium domains support the action.** Unequal bulges and intervening valleys are visible immediately behind the bases. The former four-corner frame has been replaced by a denser, irregular domain arrangement with supporting protein mass.
4. **Molecular proportions and identity are clear.** Bases are long and slender, backbones are thinner and slate colored, nucleotide colors persist, and the same template/RNA can be tracked through three arrivals.
5. **Connection and annotation defects are repaired.** The exit/RNA seam is absent. Local white letters remain legible in the sampled delivery frames. Each new unit remains part of the chain after docking.

No unresolved major blocker was found for this bounded proof. That does not automatically approve the unseen cell, packaging, export, ribosome, or folding scenes.
