## Visual contract

- Blue-violet atmospheric field, pale cyan lower region, restrained depth layers. Cell membranes pink/lilac, nucleus violet, enzymes ochre/gold, DNA/RNA backbones slate blue, amino acids violet. Base roles: A green, T red, C pale blue, G yellow, U orange. Use supported palette tokens; do not silently change the global player palette.
- Scene owns the frame. No chapter cards, headline panels, progress graphics, or permanent explanatory text inside the geometry. Sparse local labels with quiet leaders; optional original subtitles below the image.
- Genuine volumetric smooth organic shapes with broad lobes and a finer procedural finish. Single continuous shells or coherent domain assemblies; avoid random disconnected spheres posing as proteins. Thin geometry must remain clearly shaded and visible at 1280×720.
- Scale transitions locate each smaller structure in its parent. Camera travels into a cell/nucleus, follows chromatin toward DNA, approaches polymerase's open active site, follows released RNA through a pore, then reveals translation. Hold camera still enough to read docking. Preserve each biochemical object's identity within its operation.
- Overview DNA uses legible double-helix tubes and paired rods, matching the reference's abstraction; do not substitute an atom cloud. Bond/atom rendering is for a genuinely molecular structural close-up. Procedural proteins here are conceptual envelopes, not deposited atomic structures.
- Biology: strands antiparallel; RNA complementary to template, U instead of T; synthesis adds to RNA 3′ end. Nuclear export is through an envelope pore. Ribosome advances one codon, anticodons dock, peptide transfers, empty tRNA exits. Simplified tRNA glyphs and compressed molecular events must not claim realistic molecular dynamics.
