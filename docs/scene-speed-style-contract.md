# Benchmark style contract

Historical experiment record. Its prompts, style criteria, and test counts describe that experiment; current production rules are in [instruction architecture](instruction-architecture.md).

References: approved components and derivative examples in the user's 3blue1brown-explanation-style skill. Dark open mathematical space, stable concept colors, readable labels next to their objects; reject slide-title/chart/footer templates. Mechanisms must carry the reasoning. Text supports, not replaces, visible transformations.

- RNA: one DNA template, one growing complementary RNA strand, polymerase. Pair T→A, A→U, C→G, G→C at the active site. Template reads 3′→5′; RNA grows 5′→3′. Preserve the molecule between scenes. Show RNA separating while DNA remains.
- Binary search: one sorted eight-cell array. Show comparisons, then eliminate impossible ranges. Keep original positions and values. Preserve surviving interval between scenes. Make halving visible before generalizing.
- Derivative: one original x-by-x square. Add two x-by-h strips and one h-by-h corner. Relate areas to the difference quotient, then shrink h; earn 2x rather than revealing it at the start. Preserve original square and added regions across scenes.

Labels have reading holds; unrelated objects do not share confusing colors. No answer before spoken derivation, no objects hidden behind labels, no arbitrary camera changes. Render cue-adjacent and uniform samples, plus real playback for timing. Review reasoning, readability, style and technical integrity separately. A faster result with a clear downgrade is rejected.
